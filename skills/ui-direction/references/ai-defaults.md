# AI defaults

Generated interfaces cluster around a few looks whatever the subject. Each is
fine when the brief or brand asks for it. Chosen by habit, it marks the work
as generated. Keep this list current: add a look when you see it repeat.

## Whole looks

- Warm cream background (around `#F4F1EA`) with a high-contrast serif display
  and a terracotta or clay accent (around `#D97757`).
- Near-black background with one neon or acid accent and glowing edges.
- Broadsheet: hairline rules, zero radius, dense columns, italic serif
  display, small tracked monospace labels.
- The SaaS-card kit: everything in identical rounded cards, one radius on
  everything, the same soft grey shadow under each, gradient washes.
- Purple-to-blue or multicolor gradients, glows as the main affordance,
  glass and blur as decoration.

## Page scaffolds

- Three or more same-size cards of icon, heading, and text as the page
  structure. Cards nested in cards.
- On Persuade pages, the hero-metric block: big number, small label,
  supporting stats, accent. (KPI tiles are right on Operate dashboards.)
- Section numbers (01 / 02 / 03) when the content is not a sequence.
- A modal for a task that needs neither interruption nor protected focus.

## Type and copy tells

- A tracked ALL-CAPS eyebrow label above every heading.
- One word in a headline set in italic, bold, or a different color.
- Metadata joined with middle dots (`A · B · C`); labels as `WORD — fragment`.
- `→` appended to every link and button.
- Monospace for labels that are not code, data, or measurements.
- Generic copy: "Unlock the power of", "Seamless", "Revolutionize",
  "Supercharge", invented testimonials, logos, or stats, lorem ipsum.
- Sample data like "Item 1", "John Doe", "test@test.com".

## Fonts reached for by default

For a display or brand face, these mean you stopped looking: Fraunces,
Playfair Display, Cormorant, Lora, Crimson, Newsreader, Syne, Space Grotesk,
Space Mono, IBM Plex, Inter as a display face, DM Sans, DM Serif, Outfit,
Plus Jakarta Sans, Instrument Sans. They are fine as body or UI faces when
chosen on purpose, and Inter or the system stack is a sound UI face for
Operate products.

## Surface habits

- Gradient text.
- A colored `border-left` thicker than 1 px on cards, list items, or alerts.
- Hard offset shadows (`4px 4px 0`) outside a deliberately neobrutalist look.
- Tinted near-black (`#0B0B0B`, `#111`) used everywhere instead of a chosen
  ink color.
- Emoji or Unicode glyphs instead of an icon set; mixed icon sets.
- Fade-and-slide-up on every section; hover lift or motion on every card.
- Light or dark picked by category instead of by where the product is used.
- A dark mode that is the light mode with inverted colors.
- Gray text on a colored background; contrast that only works in one theme.
- Everything centered, or everything bold.
- Placeholder or broken images and default framework favicons in a finished
  build.
